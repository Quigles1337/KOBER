# R0 — the reading set the S-0001 constraint system depends on (P0.3)

- **Definition.** R0 is the enumerated reading set on which every KOBER theorem about the
  Corazza et al. 2021 constraint system is conditional (E2). It is derived entirely from the
  stored version of record (`sources/corazza-etal-2021-jas-105214.pdf`, SHA-256 in S-0001);
  page numbers are that PDF's printed pages. Edition attributions are **"as cited by S-0001"**
  (its References, p. 13–14) — KOBER has not yet consulted any edition independently; that
  cross-check is P2.2.
- **Structure.** R0 = R0-agg (the aggregate attestation matrix) + R0-named (the individually
  named readings) + the variant axes R0a–R0d. The operative baseline used by the paper's
  Simulation 2 is **R0a**.
- **Provenance rule.** Every reading below preserves the paper's damage notation verbatim.
  Nothing here asserts what any tablet "really" says; R0 records what S-0001 *used*.

## 3. R0-agg — the aggregate attestation matrix

R0-agg is Tables 1 and 2 of S-0001 (p. 2) exactly as transcribed in
`analysis/P0.2-constraint-system.md` §3 (render-verified). Basis per the captions: "the
standard corpus (Godart and Olivier, 1985) and inscriptions published later", uncertain
readings excluded; the doubtful items *180+B, *180+SA + B (MA 4) and HHH (CHIC #068.r.A)
appear in Table 1 but were **not used in Simulation 2**.

**Honest limitation (E6 spirit):** S-0001 does not decompose these counts per inscription. The
per-document expansion of R0-agg is *not recoverable from the paper alone*. Consequences:
1. KOBER theorems conditional on R0 are conditional on the matrix **as published**, including
   S-0001's editorial judgments about which readings are "uncertain".
2. Independent re-derivation of the matrix from a licensed transcription source (SigLA, S-0004,
   CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) is a defined P2.2 task; agreement or divergence with Table 1/2 is a result
   either way, and divergence would spawn additional R0 variants.

## 5. Variant enumeration

- **R0a (baseline; the paper's operative set).** KH 86.2 = A A; E J readings treated as
  doubtful (excluded); PH 7b.3 = J̣ E; L corpus per fn. 2; MA 4 / CHIC #068 items and MM II–III
  material excluded from Simulation 2. Everything in §3–§4 as stated.

- **R0b (GORILA reading of KH 86.2 = A B B).** Deltas (executor inference, machine-verification
  at P1.3/P2): Table 1 loses combination A A (1), gains A B B (1); Table 2 totals A 15→14,
  B 36→38. Frequency rank order of the 12 CP signs is **unchanged** (J 137 > E 105 > D 68 >
  B 38 > K 32 > L2 26 > F 23 > H 19 > A 14 > L3 3 > L4 2, L6 2). Constraint layer: the C4
  instance set changes (drop 2A < 1, add A + 2B < 1), but both are non-binding over the
  domains: J > A already forces A < 1/2, and with A ≤ 1/3, B ≤ 1/5 (largest domain values below
  the C5 bounds), max(A + 2B) = 1/3 + 2/5 = 11/15 < 1. **Predicted: identical Simulation-2
  solution set; the measure layer shifts** (G_AmbCombs loses/gains the A A / A B B collision
  cases; the A A = 1/12 structural argument for A = 1/24 loses its attested-combination
  status). This is the flagship P1.3 variant-sensitivity target.

- **R0c (E J genuine on HT 123a.3–4 and/or ZA 8.4).** Written order E-then-J implies E > J,
  contradicting C1+C5 (J = 1/2 > E = 1/4). Consequence: Constraint 5 cannot hold as a universal
  over the corpus; it must be re-scoped (as the paper implicitly does by writing
  "unproblematic inscriptions"). Under R0c the system as formalized with universal C5 is
  **inconsistent** — which is itself a theorem-shaped fact worth proving conditionally (E2/P1.3:
  "R0c + universal C5 → no solutions").

- **R0d (PH 7b.3 = Younger's L E).** L attestations 1→2. The §3 χ² argument survives on the
  paper's own Table 5 (hypothetical L = 2 row: p = 2×10⁻¹¹, still significant), so the
  L-exclusion is variant-robust at this margin. Also would give bare L an ordering datum
  (L > E); moot while L is excluded from the variable set.

- **Not variants but variable-set choices** (recorded for completeness): excluding W, X
  (possible identities B B, A A), Y, Ω, and L are analysis decisions of S-0001 over R0, not
  reading disagreements. A future battery may treat them as model variants (Phase 3 concern).
